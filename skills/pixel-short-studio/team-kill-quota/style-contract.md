# Team Kill Quota — style contract

- Logical canvas: 640×360 at 30 fps; 2× nearest-neighbor 1280×720 delivery.
- Effective sprite pixel size: high-density pixel clusters; operators read at 72–82 logical pixels tall.
- Palette: Tarkov-inspired muted olive, gray, black, coyote brown and desaturated forest green.
- Shading: hard 3–5 band ramps with restrained warm sunlight and cool mist.
- Outline: dark charcoal/brown pixel clusters; no smooth vector stroke.
- Perspective: cinematic side/three-quarter forest walk with a shallow foreground ground plane.
- Anchors: bottom-center for walking and standing; center/bottom-center by reaction frame for the fall.
- Walk cycle: exaggerated four-frame contact–passing–opposite-contact–opposite-passing gait at four frames per second; legs and boots visibly swing forward/back while upper-body mass, rifle, laser mount, and planted-foot baseline remain locked.
- Background: dense painterly pixel forest above a broad, continuous flat dirt clearing across the lower 42%; no logs, boulders, roots, shrubs, ledges, or other obstacles intersect the PMC walk lane.
- Lighting: baked forest daylight; engine effects are limited to hard flash, green and blue lasers, muzzle pixels and shadows.
- Text: off-white bitmap text inside dark translucent pixel speech bubbles; Discord scene uses a custom pixel UI.
- Speech-bubble safety: no bubble fill, border, shadow, or pointer may cover any character pixel. Place bubbles in negative space and terminate pointers just outside the speaker silhouette.
- Avoid: anti-aliasing, motion blur, gore, realistic wounds, photoreal sprites, smooth gradients, modern UI softness.
